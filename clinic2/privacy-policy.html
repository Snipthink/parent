Privacy Policy

This policy explains how this clinic's website handles information.

1. Information We Collect

When you use the appointment request form on this website, we ask for basic details such as your name, age, gender, reason for visit, and any optional problem details you choose to share. This information is used only to help the clinic understand and respond to your appointment request.

2. Appointment Information

The information you enter in the appointment form is not stored on this website or on any server. It is formatted into a message that you send directly through your own WhatsApp application to the clinic's WhatsApp number.

3. WhatsApp Communication

When you choose to send an appointment request, your device opens WhatsApp with a pre-filled message. That message, and any further conversation, is handled by WhatsApp under its own privacy policy and terms. This website has no control over how WhatsApp stores or transmits that conversation.

4. Website Usage

This website does not use tracking cookies or analytics scripts by default. If the clinic later adds analytics, advertising, or other third-party scripts, this policy should be updated to reflect that.

5. Data Security

Because this website does not operate a server or database, it does not store patient data. Any data security responsibility for messages sent through WhatsApp rests with WhatsApp and with the clinic's own handling of received messages.

6. Third-Party Services

This website may link to third-party services such as WhatsApp, Google Maps, Facebook, or Instagram. Each of these services has its own privacy practices, and we encourage you to review them separately.

7. Patient Rights

You may choose not to submit the appointment form and instead contact the clinic directly by phone or email, where available. You are always in control of what information you choose to share.

8. Contact

If you have questions about this privacy policy, please contact the clinic using the details provided on the Contact section of the homepage.

9. Changes to This Policy

This is a general template. The clinic operating this website should review, adapt, and keep this policy up to date to reflect its actual practices and applicable local laws.
