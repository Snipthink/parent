Agriculture & IoT

Crinit

Smart Farm to Fork

Crinit is an IoT-powered connected farming platform that bridges the gap between cultivation and consumption. It delivers real-time sensor-driven crop management, agricultural automation, and end-to-end supply chain visibility — giving farmers intelligent tools to monitor soil health, water usage, and harvest readiness, while ensuring consumers receive verified, traceable produce directly from source.

Education Technology

FutureTrack

Smart Ed-Tech Platform

FutureTrack transforms traditional paper-based education into a fully digital, guided experience. Designed to empower students, teachers, and institutions, it offers structured focus workflows, progress tracking dashboards, and measurable learning outcomes. From timed practice sessions to performance analytics, FutureTrack turns everyday study habits into data-driven growth journeys.

Civic Tech & Community

SocialMapWork

Map Based Problem Reporting System

SocialMapWork is a community-powered civic action platform that puts local issues on the map — literally. Citizens can pin, report, and track public problems like potholes, broken infrastructure, or sanitation concerns directly on an interactive map. Built around social visibility and public engagement, it creates accountability between communities and local authorities, turning passive frustration into organised, location-tagged civic action.

Interactive Map Issue Reporting Civic Engagement Community Voting Authority Alerts Status Tracking
