Product Details

Smart Focus Mode

FutureTrack keeps students focused on their phone. Smart Focus Mode reminds them to solve questions at set intervals while using the phone, so practice happens even on busy days.

It blends gentle reminders, timed sessions, and progress nudges so learners stay consistent without feeling pressured.
