🎯 Focus Mode

Your Smartphone,
Your Study Tool

Most students carry a powerful computer in their pocket but use it for entertainment. Focus Mode flips that habit — it personalises your question feed and keeps distractions away so every screen-time minute becomes study time.

  • Personalised subject & topic filter
  • Timed sessions: 10, 20, or 30 minutes
  • Adaptive difficulty — gets harder as you improve
  • Distraction blocking during active sessions
  • Daily goal reminders to build the study habit
  • 100% paperless — no notebooks needed

📚 Module Tests

Practice Faster
with Chapter Drills

Instead of attempting a full mock test every time, drill a single chapter in minutes. Regular short tests beat marathon sessions for long-term retention — and FutureTrack tracks every attempt so you see improvement clearly.

  • Every module maps to one chapter in your syllabus
  • Complete a test in a single bus ride or break
  • Instant explanation for every wrong answer
  • Reshuffled questions on retakes for true mastery
  • Module history with attempt-over-attempt comparison

  • Subject-wise accuracy breakdown
  • Week-by-week progress graph

📝 Teacher Tools

Create Once.
Print, Protect & Share.

Teachers can build their own question sets right inside the FutureTrack app — tag every question by chapter, concept and difficulty, then decide exactly how it leaves your hands.

  • Build custom MCQ & NCERT-aligned question sets by chapter and concept
  • Export a branded, ready-to-print PDF — with or without answers
  • Your name, publisher info and a scan-to-download QR code on every page
  • Protected Mode locks a set so it only opens inside FutureTrack's Daily Practice — never copied, edited, or shared elsewhere
  • Get discovered by students and parents as a Verified Teacher
