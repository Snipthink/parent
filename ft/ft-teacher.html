🎓 FutureTrack Education Research — Phase 2

Help Turn Student Screen Time Into Learning Time

FutureTrack is selecting 100 teachers across India for a 10-day research and testing program to explore how digital screen time can become meaningful learning.

FutureTrack is inviting teachers from across India to participate in Phase 2 of its education research program.

Selected teachers will test the FutureTrack App, provide initial feedback, introduce the app to selected students and their parents, collect their feedback during the testing period, and share their observations with the FutureTrack Research Team.

This is a research and product-testing program — not simply an employment opportunity.

The Research

Why This Research Matters

Students already spend significant time using smartphones and digital devices.

Can some of that screen time become meaningful learning time?

FutureTrack is researching whether everyday digital screen time can be transformed into productive educational activity such as:

Learning
Practice
Revision
Question Solving
Concept Understanding
Teacher-Guided Activities

The research is about understanding how students, teachers and parents experience this approach in real-world conditions.

10-Day Program

What Will Selected Teachers Do?

Test the App — download and explore FutureTrack
Initial Feedback — usability, learning experience, features, questions, student experience, problems encountered
Introduce Students — bring the app to selected students and their parents
Student/Parent Trial — students use the app; parents can share observations
Collect Feedback — gather relevant student and parent feedback
Final Research Feedback — share findings, suggestions and observations with the FutureTrack Research Team
Program Duration: 10 Days
Trial Participants

What Will Students & Parents Do?

Students

  • Use the FutureTrack App during the 10-day research period
  • Try learning, practice and question-solving features
  • Follow teacher-guided learning activities
  • Share their experience through the teacher

Parents

  • Are introduced to the FutureTrack App by the teacher
  • Observe their child's experience during the trial
  • Can share observations and feedback
  • Support the student's participation in the research
Student Value

How Can Students Benefit?

More Productive Screen Time — can help students use digital devices for structured learning activities
Learning & Practice — students can use the platform for practice and revision
Better Question Practice — teacher-created question sets may provide additional practice
Teacher-Guided Learning — teachers can help students use digital learning more effectively
Feedback-Based Improvement — research feedback is intended to help FutureTrack improve the student experience
Access to a Growing Ecosystem — students may participate in a global digital learning environment

These are intended outcomes of the research — FutureTrack does not guarantee academic improvement.
