Personalised Subject Filter

Choose exactly which subjects and topics matter most to you right now. Focus Mode filters your question feed so every session is targeted at your weak areas — no distractions, no wasted time.

Block Distractions

During a Focus session FutureTrack keeps you inside the app and away from social media and notifications. Your smartphone becomes a study tool, not a distraction — the way it should be.

Daily Focus Goals

Set a daily question target and let FutureTrack remind you when you haven't hit it yet. Small consistent goals compound into big results over an exam cycle.

Timed Practice Sessions

Choose a session length — 10, 20, or 30 minutes — and FutureTrack delivers questions at exam speed. Build the habit of answering under time pressure before the real exam arrives.

Difficulty Progression

FutureTrack adjusts question difficulty as you improve. Beginners start with fundamentals; advanced students get pushed with harder problems. You always feel challenged but never overwhelmed.

Zero Paper Required

Every question, every answer, every revision note lives inside the app. India's classrooms are going digital — FutureTrack makes sure your preparation keeps up.

Module Tests for Faster Question Practice

A Module Test is a short, focused practice set built around one chapter or topic. Instead of attempting a full syllabus mock test every time, students can drill a single module in minutes — making daily practice quick, measurable, and habit-forming.

Chapter-wise Question Sets

Every module maps directly to a chapter in your syllabus. Pick the chapter you studied today and immediately test your understanding with curated questions — no searching, no setup.

Quick 10–20 Question Formats

Short formats mean you can complete a module test in a single bus ride or study break. Regular small tests beat one-time marathon sessions for long-term retention.

Instant Answer Review

After each module test, FutureTrack shows which questions you got wrong and why. Explanations are clear and concise — you learn from mistakes immediately, not days later.

Repeat Until You Master It

Attempt the same module multiple times with reshuffled questions. FutureTrack tracks improvement across attempts so you know exactly when a topic is truly covered.

Performance Tracking with Score Card

Every test you take in FutureTrack adds to a running performance record. The Score Card gives you a clear, honest picture of where you stand — by subject, by module, and over time — so you always know what to work on next.

Score Card Dashboard

Your Score Card is a single screen that shows your overall accuracy, total questions attempted, time spent studying, and improvement trend — updated after every session in real time.

Subject-wise Accuracy Report

See your accuracy percentage broken down by subject. If Physics is at 85% and Chemistry at 52%, you know exactly where tomorrow's focus session should go — no guessing required.

Progress Over Time Graph

A week-by-week graph shows whether your scores are rising, plateauing, or slipping. Seeing a rising line is motivating; seeing a plateau tells you it is time to change strategy.

Weak Topic Alerts

FutureTrack automatically flags topics where your accuracy has dropped below a threshold. You get a nudge to revisit those chapters before they become a problem on exam day.

Data-Driven Preparation

FutureTrack turns raw practice into actionable insight. Use your Score Card to set realistic weekly targets and measure whether your study strategy is actually working — not just how many hours you put in.

Focus Mode — Your Smartphone, Your Study Tool

Most students carry a powerful computer in their pocket but use it for entertainment instead of education. Focus Mode in FutureTrack flips that habit. It personalises your question feed to the topics you need most and keeps distractions away — turning screen time into study time, one session at a time.

Become a Top Performer

FutureTrack exists to close the gap between how much a student studies and how well they actually perform. By combining structured module tests, real-time score feedback, and personalised focus sessions, the platform gives every student — regardless of school or coaching background — a fair shot at scoring higher. Join thousands of students who are preparing smarter, not harder.
