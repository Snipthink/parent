Terms & Conditions

1. Reservations and Seating Policy

Reservations made through AURA's website or digital ordering portal are subject to table availability. Guests are requested to arrive within 15 minutes of their scheduled booking time. After 15 minutes without prior notification, tables may be released to waiting patrons.

2. WhatsApp Ordering & Digital Requests

Orders placed via the digital menu workflow transmit directly to our restaurant front desk via encrypted WhatsApp text. Order confirmation, exact preparation timing, and payment processing are confirmed directly with our team via WhatsApp message exchange.

3. Dietary Restrictions and Allergies

While our kitchen takes extreme care to prevent cross-contamination, we operate in an open wood-fire environment where tree nuts, seafood, dairy, and gluten are handled daily. Guests with severe or life-threatening allergies must inform their server prior to ordering.
