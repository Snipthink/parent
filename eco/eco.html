Our Ecosystem

One Vision. Multiple Organizations. Shared Impact.

Our ecosystem brings together specialized organizations working toward one common mission: empowering innovation that creates measurable social and economic impact worldwide. Each organization focuses on a unique area while collaborating to transform ideas into real-world solutions.

YouthLabCo
Fort Collins, Colorado, USA · Youth Lab India · info@youthlabco.com

Entrepreneurial education platform preparing youth for startups, higher education, and high-paying careers.

Why

Many young people never get early, hands-on exposure to entrepreneurship, career planning, or real-world problem solving before they have to choose a path.

How

Through startup bootcamps, mentorship, and career-mapping programs delivered directly in schools and communities across Colorado and India.

What

Entrepreneurship and innovation programs, STEM and soft-skills classes, and career-mapping tools that connect students into the wider ecosystem's mentor and accelerator network.

Perception of India
perceptionofindia.com

Changing how the world experiences India — culture, tourism, and global branding.

Why

India's global image is often reduced to a handful of stereotypes that don't reflect its full economic, cultural, and diplomatic reality.

How

Through storytelling, cultural diplomacy, tourism campaigns, and media partnerships that reach international audiences directly.

What

Culture and tourism content, nation-branding campaigns, and international-relations initiatives that connect global audiences with a fuller picture of India.

Visit Official Website
IGATT
Fort Collins, Colorado, USA · info@igatt.org

Institute for Global Agriculture & Technology Transfer — AgTech education, food security, and climate-smart enterprise.

Why

Smallholder farmers and food systems worldwide face growing pressure from climate change, water scarcity, and slow technology adoption.

How

Through applied research, technology-transfer partnerships, and climate-smart training delivered directly to farming communities.

What

Food-security programs, agricultural technology transfer, water and energy management projects, and climate-adaptation research based out of Fort Collins, Colorado.

Visit Official Website
InnoP Global
Greeley, Colorado, USA · info@innopglobal.com

Innovative-Preneur accelerator helping AgTech, Biotech, CleanTech & AI startups scale globally.

Why

Early-stage founders, especially outside major hubs, often lack access to mentors, capital, and go-to-market expertise.

How

Through a structured accelerator model pairing founders with experienced mentors, boot camps, and a global investor network.

What

Startup incubation and acceleration in AgTech, Biotech, CleanTech, IoT, AI and Big Data, based in Greeley, Colorado with global reach.

Visit Official Website
DINNTEK

Sustainable technology transfer for clean energy, water, food, and healthier communities.

Why

Sustainable technologies often stay stuck in the lab instead of reaching the communities and enterprises that need them most.

How

Through hands-on consulting and technology-transfer partnerships that translate research into practical, appropriate solutions.

What

Water, food and energy sustainability projects, technology education, and enterprise consulting aimed at healthier, more resilient communities.
