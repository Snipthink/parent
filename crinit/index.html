Smart Farm

Complete farm automation customized to your land, crop, and scale

💨

Aeroponic System

Soil-less growing with nutrient mist. 90% less water usage, 3x faster growth. Perfect for leafy greens, herbs, and strawberries.

  • Mist chamber with timer control
  • Nutrient pH/EC auto-dosing
  • Root zone temperature control
  • Growth cycle monitoring
💧

Hydroponic System

Water-based nutrient delivery. NFT, DWC, and Ebb & Flow systems. Ideal for tomatoes, peppers, lettuce, and herbs.

  • Automated nutrient mixing
  • Water circulation pump control
  • pH/EC continuous monitoring
  • Reservoir management
🌾

Drip Irrigation

Precision water delivery for field crops. Soil moisture-triggered irrigation with zone-based control for maximum water efficiency.

  • Soil moisture triggered valves
  • Multi-zone scheduling
  • Fertigation integration
  • Weather-adaptive watering
🤖

AI Farm Manager

Central brain that connects everything. Crop planning, disease prediction, yield forecasting, and automated decision making.

  • Crop lifecycle management
  • Disease early warning
  • Yield prediction models
  • Automated controls
