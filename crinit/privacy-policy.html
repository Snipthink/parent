01.Information We Collect

To provide the Crinit Business App and related services (farm business calculator, interactive farm map, organic farming guides, IoT/Cloud dashboards, and Crinit Fresh ordering where applicable), we may collect:

  • Account information — name, email address, and phone number used to create and secure your account.
  • Farm & usage data — farm locations you add, investment/cost entries, crop details, and in-app activity needed to power the calculator, map, and analytics features.
  • Device sensor/IoT data — readings from connected Crinit IoT kits (pH, EC, temperature, humidity, and other agricultural parameters) where you have linked a device.
  • Device & diagnostic information — device model, operating system version, app version, and crash/diagnostic logs used to keep the app stable.
  • Approximate location — only where you choose to place a farm marker on the interactive map; we do not track your location in the background.

We do not knowingly collect more data than is necessary to operate the features you actively use.

02.How We Use Your Information

  • To create and maintain your Crinit account and sync your farm/business data across sessions.
  • To calculate farm investment totals, cost breakdowns, and display your farms on the interactive map.
  • To provide crop health insights, organic farming guidance, and fertilizer recipe calculations inside the app.
  • To operate Crinit Cloud dashboards and process data from connected IoT sensor kits.
  • To respond to support requests sent to our contact email.
  • To detect, prevent, and fix technical issues, and to improve app performance and reliability.

We do not sell your personal data to third parties.

03.Data Sharing & Third Parties

We only share data where necessary to operate the service:

  • With cloud hosting and infrastructure providers who process data on our behalf, under confidentiality obligations.
  • With the Google Play Store for app distribution, billing, and diagnostics, subject to Google's own policies.
  • When required by law, legal process, or to protect the rights, safety, and property of SnipThinK, our users, or the public.
  • With your explicit consent, for any purpose not covered above.

04.Data Retention

We retain your personal data only for as long as your account is active or as needed to provide you the app's features, comply with our legal obligations, resolve disputes, and enforce our agreements.

05.Data & Account Deletion Policy

In line with Google Play's Developer Program Policies and Data Safety requirements, Crinit provides users with clear ways to request deletion of their account and associated data.

Request deletion by email. You can request full deletion of your account and personal data at any time by emailing us at snipthink@gmail.com with the subject line "Data Deletion Request", using the email address or account details registered with Crinit. We verify the request and permanently delete the associated account data within 30 days, except where retention is required by law.

Automatic deletion after account deletion. If you delete your account from within the app (where available) or your deletion request is processed, your personal data is scheduled for permanent removal. Any residual data kept in backups for continuity purposes is purged within 90 days.

Automatic deletion for inactive accounts. If an account remains inactive for 90 consecutive days after a deletion request is initiated (or, for accounts flagged inactive without further action from the user), we automatically and permanently delete the account and its associated personal data from our active systems.

Data that we are required to retain for legal, security, fraud-prevention, tax, or accounting purposes may be kept in a de-identified or archived form for the minimum period required by applicable law, even after account deletion.

06.Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you.
  • Correct inaccurate or incomplete data.
  • Request deletion of your data, as described above.
  • Withdraw consent for optional data processing (such as location on the farm map).
  • Object to or restrict certain processing of your data.

To exercise any of these rights, contact us using the details in the "Contact Us" section below.

07.Data Security

We use reasonable administrative, technical, and physical safeguards to protect your data against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

08.Children's Privacy

Crinit is intended for farmers, agricultural businesses, and adult users. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal data, contact us and we will delete it promptly.

09.Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices, features, or legal requirements. Material changes will be reflected by updating the "Effective date" above. Continued use of Crinit after changes take effect constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
