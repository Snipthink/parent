2. No Fees to Job Seekers

Kaarigar never charges job seekers to browse, search, or apply for jobs. Applications are submitted directly to each employer's own Google Form. If anyone claiming to represent Kaarigar asks you for money, a "processing fee," or your banking details to "release" a job offer, it is not us, and you should report it immediately using the Stay in Touch button in the footer below.

Always confirm details directly with the hiring company before relying on them.

4. Assessments
