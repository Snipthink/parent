Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 20, 2026

1. What This Policy Covers

This Privacy Policy explains what happens to information when you use the Kaarigar website. Kaarigar is a static, client-side site: job, category and company data loads directly into your browser from files we publish, and there is no Kaarigar server or database receiving your browsing activity.

2. Information We Do Not Collect

Kaarigar does not run its own application backend. We do not collect your resume, CSV exports, contact details, or job-application content — those are sent by your browser directly to the employer's own Google Form when you click "Apply Now," and are governed by Google's privacy policy and the employer's own data practices, not ours.

3. Local Device Storage

The site stores one small preference — your light/dark theme choice — in your browser's local storage, purely so the site remembers your preference on return visits. This never leaves your device and is not shared with us.

4. Information Employers Receive

When you apply to a listing, whatever you submit on that employer's Google Form (e.g. name, contact details, resume CSV) is received and controlled by that employer, not by Kaarigar. Please review the linked form and the employer's own privacy practices before submitting personal information.

5. Cookies & Analytics

Kaarigar does not set tracking cookies or run third-party advertising trackers. If we introduce aggregate, privacy-respecting analytics in the future, this policy will be updated accordingly.

6. Children's Privacy

Kaarigar is intended for use by working-age adults seeking employment and is not directed at children.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may revise this Policy from time to time; the "Last updated" date above will reflect the most recent change.

8. Contact

Privacy questions can be sent to us using the Stay in Touch button in the footer below.
