Applicant guide

From resume to job offer, no agents, no fees

Everything you need to apply for a job on Kaarigar — start to finish.

1

Download the Future Track app

Future Track is a free resume-builder app that works completely offline. Build your work history, skills, education and certifications once — it stays on your device and you can reuse it for every job you apply to on Kaarigar.

2

Export your resume as a CSV

Once your profile is complete, use Future Track's "Export" option to generate a single CSV file containing your resume data — formatted exactly the way employers on Kaarigar expect it. Save it somewhere easy to find (Downloads, Google Drive, etc).

3

Find a job and open its application form

Use the Find Jobs page — filter by category, country, experience and salary band. Open any listing's Know More page, then click "Apply Now." Each job links to its own dedicated Google Form, run directly by the hiring company.

4

Upload your CSV and submit

On the Google Form, fill in any job-specific questions and upload the resume CSV you exported in step 2. Submit the form — that's your application sent directly to the employer.

5

Wait for the company to respond

The employer reviews submissions directly from their Google Form. If you're shortlisted, they'll reach out using the contact details from your resume. Kaarigar doesn't sit in the middle of this — there's no agent, no recruiter fee, and no application tracking on our end.

A few honest notes

  • Some listings may require an assessment before you're shortlisted — look for the Assessment Needed tag on the job card or detail page.
  • Jobs marked Visa Sponsored mean the employer has indicated they'll support a work visa — always confirm details directly with the company.
  • Kaarigar never asks you to pay to apply for a job. If anyone claiming to represent Kaarigar asks for money, it's not us. See our Terms & Conditions for details.
