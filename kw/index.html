          Initial budget estimates are provided during booking. Final costs are agreed upon before work commences.

          Customers can cancel or reschedule bookings free of charge before the service partner departs for the location.

          All service professionals undergo background verification. Customers are advised to secure personal valuables.

          Privacy Policy

          Last updated: August 2026

          At Kaamwale, we prioritize your privacy and data security. This policy outlines how we collect, use, and protect your information.

          1. Information We Collect

          We collect information provided by you during service booking, such as your selected category, timing preferences, budget, GPS coordinates, manual address, and issue description.

          2. How We Use Your Data

          Your details are used solely to match you with a nearby verified service professional and generate your WhatsApp booking message.

          3. Location Data

          GPS location is captured only when you explicitly click the 'Share GPS location' button to help technicians reach your destination accurately.

          4. Data Protection & Contact

          We employ strict security measures to protect your data. If you have privacy inquiries, contact us at privacy@kaamwale.example.
