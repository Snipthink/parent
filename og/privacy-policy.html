Privacy Policy

Last updated: 24 August 2026

1. Introduction

Organicabelle ("we", "us", "our") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect when you visit this website or contact us, how we use it, and the choices you have. By using this site, you agree to the practices described here.

2. Information We Collect

We collect information you choose to give us, such as:

  • Your name, when you use our Contact form or place an order.
  • The message, complaint, or order details you type into a form.
  • Your phone number, if you contact us on WhatsApp.

We do not run analytics or advertising trackers on this site, and we do not knowingly collect information beyond what you actively submit to us.

3. How We Use Your Information

  • To respond to your enquiries, complaints, and bulk order requests.
  • To process and fulfil orders placed via WhatsApp.
  • To improve our products and customer service.

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third parties.

4. How Orders & Messages Are Sent

This website does not store form submissions on a server. When you submit the Contact form or place an order, your browser opens WhatsApp with a pre-filled message addressed to our business number — the message is only sent once you press send inside WhatsApp, and from that point on it is handled under WhatsApp's own privacy policy.

5. Data Retention

Any conversation history is retained only within WhatsApp, according to your own device and WhatsApp account settings. We keep records of orders and complaints for as long as reasonably necessary to fulfil them and meet accounting/legal requirements.

6. Your Rights

You may ask us to access, correct, or delete personal information you have shared with us by writing to the email address below.

7. Children's Privacy

Our products and this website are intended for adults. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date.
