Privacy Policy

FutureTrack by SnipThink is a health-tech awareness application. This Privacy Policy explains how information may be collected, used, stored, and protected when people use FutureTrack websites, mobile applications, scanner tools, reports, and related services.

Information Collected

FutureTrack may collect information such as name, age, gender, height, weight, lifestyle answers, food scan history, app usage data, device details, crash logs, support messages, and optional account or contact details. Users should avoid submitting unnecessary sensitive information.

Camera Permission and Image Processing

Camera permission is used to scan food packets, nutrition labels, ingredient lists, and product information. Images may be processed on-device or through trusted systems to perform OCR, ingredient analysis, and educational report generation.

Analytics, Cookies, and Third Parties

FutureTrack may use analytics, security, crash-reporting, hosting, and performance services. Websites may use cookies or similar technologies for basic functionality, analytics, and service improvement.

Data Storage and Security

Reasonable technical and organizational safeguards are used to protect information. No internet service can guarantee absolute security, so users should verify important food and health decisions independently.

User Rights

Depending on location, users may request access, correction, deletion, portability, objection, or withdrawal of consent. FutureTrack considers GDPR-style privacy rights, Indian DPDP Act principles, and worldwide privacy expectations where applicable.

Children's Privacy

FutureTrack is designed for education and awareness. Children should use the service with parent or guardian involvement. Parents may contact FutureTrack about children's data concerns.

Contact

For privacy requests, contact: support.futuretrack@gmail.com
