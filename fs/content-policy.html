Content Policy

FutureTrack promotes health awareness, food education, and responsible discussion. Content must not mislead users, make guaranteed medical claims, or present app results as diagnosis, treatment, or disease prediction.

Allowed Educational Content

Users and partners may share food-awareness information, product education, general lifestyle guidance, and community recommendations when they are respectful, lawful, and not presented as guaranteed medical advice.

Disallowed Content

  • Misleading medical claims or guaranteed disease predictions
  • Abusive, hateful, threatening, or harassing submissions
  • Harmful instructions or illegal activities
  • Intellectual property infringement
  • Spam, manipulation, or deceptive product promotion

User Responsibility

Users remain responsible for their submissions and decisions. FutureTrack may moderate, remove, restrict, or review content to protect the quality and safety of the platform.
