FAQ

Questions, answered

Still unsure? Talk to our team directly.

How long does the battery really last? +

Up to 6 months on standard tracking mode, depending on how frequently location updates are refreshed.

Does it work without WiFi or mobile data? +

Yes. Kavach uses GPS, Bluetooth and WiFi together, so it keeps tracking even in low-connectivity areas.

Can multiple family members see the same tag? +

Yes, the Family plan supports unlimited members viewing the same devices from their own phones.

Is the tag water resistant? +

Yes, every Kavach tag is IP67 rated, built for rain, sweat, and daily wear.

How does the SOS button work? +

A press instantly shares live location with your chosen emergency contacts, along with a notification.
